I grew up around my parents' business, so I learned early how much work can go into something that customers still never end up seeing.

A video I posted on TikTok once brought in over 100K in sales. It wasn't complicated. It just reached the right people, and after that I couldn't stop noticing how many businesses are doing everything right and still getting ignored. So now I build the websites that fix that, the ones that bring customers in instead of just sitting there looking nice.

Most sites I come across are gorgeous and completely silent. I want to build the ones that actually earn something for the person who owns them.

And when I take on a project, I care about the marketing as much as the code. Knowing who a site is for and how people actually find it changes what belongs on the page. Fashion and beauty are where I have the most fun with it.
